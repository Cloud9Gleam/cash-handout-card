# Cash handout card — poidh #1510

Business-card-sized handout (3.5 x 2 in, 1050 x 600 px at 300 dpi) on the
benefits of using and accepting cash. Black and white only, text and image
on front and back, print-ready.

## Front — `card_front.png`
"CASH IS FREEDOM" with a coin mark. Private • Fungible • Offline.
No bank needed. No tracking. It works when the power goes out.

## Back — `card_back.png`
"WHY CASH MATTERS": every card tap leaves a trail — where you were, what
you bought, who you paid. Cash leaves none. Cash is yours to hold, spend,
and give — no permission, no account, no shutdown switch.
Call to action: PAY CASH. KEEP CASH ALIVE. Use it. Ask for it. Accept it.

## Print
Print at 3.5 x 2 inches, black ink on white card stock, double-sided
(flip on long edge). No colour, no grey — pure black and white.

Original work, generated with Python/PIL (`gen_card.py`) for poidh bounty
#1510 ("Create business card sized handout on the benefits of
using/accepting cash", 0.002 ETH, deadline 2026-11-07).
